This repository contains a comprehensive data analysis and interactive dashboard project focusing on a **Bike Sales Survey**. The primary objective of this project is to clean, process, and analyse customer survey data to unearth valuable insights regarding bike-purchasing behaviour, demographic influences, and commuting patterns.

---

* **`Bike sales survey cleaned Dataset & Dshboard.xlsx`**: The processed workbook containing the cleaned data, pivot tables, and the interactive dashboard.
* **`Bike sales survey raw & uncleaned Dataset.csv`**: The initial raw dataset prior to data wrangling and standardisation.
* **`Formal_Project_Report_Bike_Buyers.docx`**: A structured formal report outlining the methodology, analytical workflow, and key business findings.
* **`dashboard_preview.png`**: Visual snapshot of the interactive Excel dashboard.

## 🛠️ Methodology & Technical Workflow

1. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing (`Bike sales survey raw & uncleaned Dataset.csv`):**
   * Addressed missing values and standardized categorical text entries across marital status, education, and regions.
   * Engineered age bracket categories (`adults`, `mature adult`, `middle age`, `senior citizen`, `young adults`) to facilitate granular demographic segmentation.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):**
   * Evaluated purchasing preferences based on commute distances (measured in miles).
   * Analysed the relationship between home ownership, car ownership, and bike purchases.
   * Investigated educational attainment levels in correlation with purchasing choices.
3. **Dashboard & Reporting:**
   * Constructed dynamic Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts in Microsoft Excel.
   * Integrated multiple interactive slicers (`age categories`, `no. of cars`, `Purchased Bike`, `Marital Status`, `Education`, `Region`, `Home Owner`, `Gender`) to allow custom multi-variable filtering.

---

## 💡 Key Insights Drawn

* **Commute Distance Impact:** Customers with shorter commutes (**0-1 Miles**) represent the highest share of bike purchasers, exhibiting a significant drop-off as commute distance increases.
* **Age Demographics:** The `mature adult` and `adults` brackets form the largest consumer segments, recording the highest overall volume of bike purchases.
* **Housing & Lifestyle:** Homeowners demonstrate distinct purchasing frequencies compared to non-homeowners, serving as a reliable indicator for targeted marketing campaigns.
